# EFESO knowledge governance

Member UX stays on `/expertise`. Provenance, confidentiality, classification, and approval stay in backend/admin.

## Visibility

`public` · `efeso_member` · `restricted_account_team` · `admin_only`

Members read only `public` and `efeso_member` objects that are approved for display, without commercial terms, and with confidentiality `public` or `internal`.

## Confidentiality

`public` · `internal` · `confidential` · `highly_confidential`

## Knowledge nature and outcome status

- `proposed_scope` is never a delivered result.
- `expected_benefit` is never `delivered`.
- Potential saving is never captured saving.
- A proposal does not prove hiring or completion.
- Client-reported need is not global catalog fact.
- Historical benchmark is not a promise.
- Another client's result is not an expected result for the current account.
- PrimeTwin content is benchmark/reference, not EFESO capability proof.

## Matching

Restricted objects may influence EFESO Fit only when `approved_for_matching=true`. The member explanation is sanitized and never includes source IDs, prices, contacts, or proposal text.

## Origins

`public_official` · `curated_internal` · `restricted_internal` · `demo_seed`

When curated catalog objects exist, `demo_seed` is archived or deprioritized and does not sustain production Fit.
